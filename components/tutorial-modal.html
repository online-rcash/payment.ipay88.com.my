System Guide

How to Make a Payment

Please follow the guide below before continuing.

01

Fill In Payment Information

Enter your full name, MyKad number, phone number, DJ/CUST number and payment amount accurately.

02

Confirm Information

Make sure all provided information is accurate before continuing. If there is a mistake, use the Back button to make corrections.

03

Choose Payment Method

Choose an available payment method and select the bank or e-wallet you want to use. Currently, payment via DuitNow is available for transactions.

04

Scan the QR Code

Open your banking or e-wallet app and scan the QR code shown. Follow the instructions in the app to complete the transaction.

05

Upload Proof of Payment

After a successful payment, upload your proof of payment or transaction receipt for verification purposes.

Security Notice

Make sure all payments are made only through the official R-Cash website. Do not share personal information, banking details, or verification codes with any unauthorized party.
